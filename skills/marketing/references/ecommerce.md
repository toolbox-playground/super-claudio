# Ecommerce Content: TikTok Shop & Instagram Shop

## TikTok Shop Content Strategy

### Video format that converts
- Hook in first 2 seconds (problem, surprise, or "watch this")
- Show product in use — not just the product itself
- Include price/offer on screen
- End with strong CTA: "Shop in the link below" / "Tap the cart icon"
- Duration: 15-30 seconds optimal for shop content

### Full TikTok Shop Video Workflow

1. **Create product-in-action video:**
   - If you have the product: film a 30-second demo
   - AI approach: Nano Banana 2 product scene → Figma Weave animation → ffmpeg voiceover

2. **Edit in CapCut:**
   - Auto-captions → font: bold, high contrast
   - Add product sticker (TikTok Shop feature)
   - Add background music (trending audio from TikTok library)

3. **Upload:**
   - Add product tag from your TikTok Shop catalog
   - Write caption with 3-5 relevant hashtags
   - Post during peak hours (7-9pm local time typically)

### GMV Max — TikTok Shop's Native AI Ad Engine (2026 Updates)

GMV Max (TikTok Shop's automated ads product) added a **Creative Hub** and a native **AI Video Generator** in 2026: upload product images and TikTok generates video content complete with background music and voiceover automatically — no filming or external tool required. GMV Max advertisers report up to 20% incremental GMV lift, and top cross-border sellers are producing 20+ ad variants per week using it. Newer capabilities are pulling affiliate/creator traffic, coupons, and platform fees into the same ROI optimization system.

1. Go to TikTok Shop Seller Center → Ads → GMV Max
2. Upload your product catalog/images
3. Use the built-in AI Video Generator for auto music + voiceover, or upload your own creative via Creative Hub
4. Let GMV Max auto-optimize bidding across ads, LIVE, and affiliate/creator traffic toward your GMV goal

Best for: sellers who want a fully native TikTok Shop path (creative + bidding + affiliate) without leaving Seller Center. Pair with Creatify or Topview AI (see video-ads.md) if you want more creative control over hooks/scripts before feeding assets in.

## Instagram Shop

- Product tags in feed posts, Reels, and Stories
- Reels with product tag perform best
- Use same video as TikTok (9:16 format works for both)
- Add product sticker in Stories

### 2026 Updates
Meta is rolling out AI-powered shopping features (announced at Shoptalk 2026): AI-generated review summaries and product/pricing details shown when a user clicks a product or ad, in-app checkout via Stripe/PayPal partnerships (no need to leave the app), and Live Video Ads expanding to Instagram (and globally on Facebook) so live shopping streams can run as paid ads.

## Content Volume for Performance

Running paid ads? Create at least 5 different creative variations to test:
- Different hooks (question, bold claim, demonstration)
- Different voiceover styles (energetic vs. calm)
- Different backgrounds/scenes
- Same product, different contexts

Use Creatify.ai or the video-ads workflow to generate variations quickly. (Note: aicreator.co is defunct as of May 2026 — do not use.)
