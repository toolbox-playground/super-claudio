# Ecommerce Content: TikTok Shop & Instagram Shop

## TikTok Shop Content Strategy

### Video format that converts
- Hook in first 2 seconds (problem, surprise, or "watch this")
- Show product in use — not just the product itself
- Include price/offer on screen
- End with strong CTA: "Shop in the link below" / "Tap the cart icon"
- Duration: 15-30 seconds optimal for shop content

### Full TikTok Shop Video Workflow

1. **Create product-in-action video:**
   - If you have the product: film a 30-second demo
   - AI approach: Nano Banana 2 product scene → Figma Weave animation → ffmpeg voiceover

2. **Edit in CapCut:**
   - Auto-captions → font: bold, high contrast
   - Add product sticker (TikTok Shop feature)
   - Add background music (trending audio from TikTok library)

3. **Upload:**
   - Add product tag from your TikTok Shop catalog
   - Write caption with 3-5 relevant hashtags
   - Post during peak hours (7-9pm local time typically)

## Instagram Shop

- Product tags in feed posts, Reels, and Stories
- Reels with product tag perform best
- Use same video as TikTok (9:16 format works for both)
- Add product sticker in Stories

## Content Volume for Performance

Running paid ads? Create at least 5 different creative variations to test:
- Different hooks (question, bold claim, demonstration)
- Different voiceover styles (energetic vs. calm)
- Different backgrounds/scenes
- Same product, different contexts

Use Creatify.ai or the video-ads workflow to generate variations quickly. (Note: aicreator.co is defunct as of May 2026 — do not use.)

## TikTok Shop's Own AI Toolkit (2026) — Free for Sellers

TikTok Shop added free, built-in AI tools for merchants (menswear/womenswear categories first, desktop + mobile app):

- **AI Fashion Video Maker** — turns still product photos into shoppable short-form videos with visuals, music, and "multi-emotion voiceovers" (uses the seller's own voice tone + lip-sync) in minutes — no model, photoshoot, or editing skills needed
- **AI Dubbing** — auto-generates localized/revised video scripts from product info and re-voices existing videos in the seller's synthetic voice with lip-sync
- **List with AI** — turns a single product image + short description into a full TikTok Shop listing (title, description, category)

Access: directly inside the TikTok Shop Seller Center app — no external tool needed for sellers in supported categories. For categories or workflows it doesn't cover (non-fashion products, cross-platform ads), still use Creatify.ai, Nano Banana 2 → Figma Weave, or the video-ads workflow above.
