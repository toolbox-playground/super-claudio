# Animating Static Images

Tools that take a still image and add realistic motion to it.

## Weavy AI (now Figma Weave) — Motion from Photo

Adds realistic motion to a static photograph (person moves, fabric flows, hair blows).

> **Rebrand note:** Figma acquired Weavy and relaunched it as **Figma Weave** (weave.figma.com).
> The standalone weavy.ai product is being folded into Figma — access now runs through a paid
> Figma plan rather than as an independent free-standing tool. If weavy.ai no longer works for
> you, use weave.figma.com or re-check `find-ai-tools` for the current free alternative.

- Great for: fashion images, lifestyle photos, product ads where a model wears/uses the product
- Input: photo + motion direction prompt
- Output: short looping or progressive video clip
- Common use case: animate an ecommerce model photo → use in TikTok Shop

**Workflow:**
1. Prepare a clean product/model photo
2. Upload to Figma Weave (weave.figma.com) or weavy.ai
3. Describe the desired motion: "model turns and smiles", "dress flows in wind", "product rotates slowly"
4. Generate and download as MP4

## Kling "Image to Video" — High Quality Motion

Kling's image-to-video mode is also excellent for animating photos. Current flagship is Kling 3.0 (Turbo/Omni variants added 4K editing and longer clips, Jun 2026), with Kling 3.5 also in rotation for professional motion-graphics work.

- URL: klingai.com → Image to Video tab
- Higher quality than many tools
- Supports longer clips from a single image (up to 15s native, extendable to ~3 min via Video Extension)

## Runway "Act One" / Gen-4.5

Runway's flagship model is now **Gen-4.5** (image-to-video and text-to-video; additional input modes rolling out) — Gen-4 remains available. Gen-4.5 ranks #1 on the Artificial Analysis Text-to-Video Arena as of Aug 2026.

- URL: runwayml.com
- Professional-grade animation from images
- More control over motion style and duration

## When to Use Which

| Tool | Best For |
|------|---------|
| Figma Weave (formerly Weavy AI) | Fashion/lifestyle/ecommerce model motion |
| Kling Image-to-Video | General high-quality animation |
| Runway | Professional production, fine control |
