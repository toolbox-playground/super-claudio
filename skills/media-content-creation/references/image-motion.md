# Animating Static Images

Tools that take a still image and add realistic motion to it.

## Figma Weave (formerly Weavy AI) — Motion from Photo

Weavy was acquired by Figma (announced Oct 2025, ~$200M) and relaunched as **Figma Weave**, a node-based AI canvas that chains image generation, motion/video, and compositing across multiple models (Google, Kling, OpenAI, ByteDance, Black Forest Labs, Runway, Luma, and more) in one workspace. Still adds realistic motion to a static photograph (person moves, fabric flows, hair blows).

- URL: weave.figma.com
- Great for: fashion images, lifestyle photos, product ads where a model wears/uses the product
- Input: photo + motion direction prompt
- Output: short looping or progressive video clip
- Common use case: animate an ecommerce model photo → use in TikTok Shop
- Pricing: free tier (limited) + paid plans ~$19–$48/mo

**Workflow:**
1. Prepare a clean product/model photo
2. Upload to Figma Weave (weave.figma.com)
3. Describe the desired motion: "model turns and smiles", "dress flows in wind", "product rotates slowly"
4. Generate and download as MP4

## Kling 3.0 "Image to Video" — High Quality Motion

Kling's image-to-video mode is also excellent for animating photos. Kling 3.0 ("Omni One" architecture, launched June 17, 2026) adds native 4K editing and a Multi-Shot feature for up to six connected camera shots from a single image/prompt.

- URL: klingai.com → Image to Video tab
- Higher quality than many tools; native 4K output
- Supports longer clips from a single image, plus multi-shot sequences

## Runway Gen-4.5 — Professional-Grade Motion

Runway's Gen-3 models (which powered the "Act One" feature) were retired July 8, 2026. The current flagship is Gen-4.5 — Runway's top-rated video model (#1 on the Artificial Analysis Text-to-Video benchmark, ~1247 Elo) — offering Image-to-Video and Text-to-Video with improved character/world consistency and up to 4K output.

- URL: runwayml.com
- Professional-grade animation from images
- More control over motion style and duration; native 4K support

## When to Use Which

| Tool | Best For |
|------|---------|
| Figma Weave (formerly Weavy AI) | Fashion/lifestyle/ecommerce model motion |
| Kling 3.0 Image-to-Video | General high-quality animation |
| Runway Gen-4.5 | Professional production, fine control |
