# Animating Static Images

Tools that take a still image and add realistic motion to it.

## Weavy AI — Motion from Photo

> **Note:** Weavy was acquired by Figma (announced Oct/Nov 2025) and is being folded into Figma as "Figma Weave" — a node-based canvas combining multiple AI image/video models. Per Figma's own statement, Weavy continues operating independently during the transition, so weavy.ai may still be the current access point — verify which URL is live before relying on this.

Adds realistic motion to a static photograph (person moves, fabric flows, hair blows).

- Great for: fashion images, lifestyle photos, product ads where a model wears/uses the product
- Input: photo + motion direction prompt
- Output: short looping or progressive video clip
- Common use case: animate an ecommerce model photo → use in TikTok Shop

**Workflow:**
1. Prepare a clean product/model photo
2. Upload to Weavy AI
3. Describe the desired motion: "model turns and smiles", "dress flows in wind", "product rotates slowly"
4. Generate and download as MP4

## Kling "Image to Video" — High Quality Motion

Kling's image-to-video mode is also excellent for animating photos. Current flagship is **Kling 3.0 Turbo / Omni** (Feb 2026) — multi-shot clips up to 15s, native audio, character consistency, up to 4K output.

- URL: klingai.com → Image to Video tab
- Higher quality than many tools
- Supports longer clips from a single image

## Runway Gen-4.5 (formerly "Act One" / Gen-3)

- URL: runwayml.com
- Runway's current flagship video model is **Gen-4.5** (announced Dec 1, 2025) — tops the Artificial Analysis Video Arena ranking, stronger physics/motion/prompt adherence, image-to-video supported
- Professional-grade animation from images; "Act One" performance-transfer capability has likely been extended under **Act-Two** (maps a driving video's face/hand/body motion onto an AI character) — verify current naming at runwayml.com
- More control over motion style and duration

## When to Use Which

| Tool | Best For |
|------|---------|
| Weavy AI | Fashion/lifestyle/ecommerce model motion |
| Kling Image-to-Video | General high-quality animation |
| Runway | Professional production, fine control |
