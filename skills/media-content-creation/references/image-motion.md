# Animating Static Images

Tools that take a still image and add realistic motion to it.

## Figma Weave (formerly Weavy AI) — Motion from Photo

**Update (verified Aug 2026): Weavy AI was acquired by Figma in October 2025 and is no longer available as a standalone product — it now ships as Figma Weave.** The node-based canvas and image-to-video workflow described below still work, just under the new name/URL.

Adds realistic motion to a static photograph (person moves, fabric flows, hair blows). Figma Weave's image-to-video runs on a mix of underlying models (Kling, Runway, Luma, Wan) behind one canvas.

- Great for: fashion images, lifestyle photos, product ads where a model wears/uses the product
- Input: photo + motion direction prompt
- Output: short looping or progressive video clip
- Common use case: animate an ecommerce model photo → use in TikTok Shop
- URL: weave.figma.com (was weavy.ai)

**Workflow:**
1. Prepare a clean product/model photo
2. Upload to Figma Weave (weave.figma.com)
3. Describe the desired motion: "model turns and smiles", "dress flows in wind", "product rotates slowly"
4. Generate and download as MP4

## Kling "Image to Video" — High Quality Motion

Kling's image-to-video mode is also excellent for animating photos.

- URL: klingai.com → Image to Video tab
- Higher quality than many tools
- Supports longer clips from a single image

## Runway Gen-4.5 / Act-Two

- URL: runwayml.com
- Professional-grade animation from images; Gen-4.5 (current flagship, supersedes Gen-3) handles Image-to-Video with higher fidelity, physics, and consistency
- Act-Two (successor to Act One) adds character/performance control, including changing a performance's voice from within the same interface
- More control over motion style and duration than most competitors

## When to Use Which

| Tool | Best For |
|------|---------|
| Figma Weave (was Weavy AI) | Fashion/lifestyle/ecommerce model motion |
| Kling Image-to-Video | General high-quality animation |
| Runway Gen-4.5 / Act-Two | Professional production, fine control |
