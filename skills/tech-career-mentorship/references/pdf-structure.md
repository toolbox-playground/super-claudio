# Mentorship PDF Structure

Use this structure as a compact, shareable working report. Aim for two pages; use a third only when validated course links or session updates cannot fit legibly. Do not shrink the type or remove context needed to understand a recommendation just to force fewer pages.

## Header

- **Title:** Plano de desenvolvimento profissional
- **Mentee:** name as supplied, or omit if not supplied
- **Updated:** current date
- **Stage:** Diagnóstico inicial / Atualização da mentoria / Fechamento
- **Target direction:** mentee's stated goal, or “A confirmar com o mentorando”

Use a quiet neutral visual style, strong text contrast, readable body type, clear section headings, and clickable source links. No logo. Avoid decorative graphics that consume space.

## Page 1 — Career snapshot

### Current profile

One short paragraph covering the person's evidenced experience, main areas, platforms/technologies, and relevant career progression. Mention employer names only when they help explain experience. Do not reproduce a full CV.

### Assessment

| Area | Current evidence | Reading / question |
|---|---|---|
| Relevant experience | Role, project, or responsibility supported by CV/LinkedIn/notes | What this demonstrates or what needs confirmation |
| Strengths | Concrete technologies, outcomes, or responsibilities | Why it is useful for the stated target |
| Scope estimate | Autonomy, complexity, and impact visible in examples | Tentative estimate; explain evidence and uncertainty |
| Career direction | Mentee-stated goal | If absent, mark “to confirm” |

Keep experience, inference, and open questions explicit. “No evidence in the CV” does not mean “does not know.”

## Page 2 — Technical development

For an initial report, label this section **Preliminary gaps to validate**. For later reports, use **Prioritized development plan** and make clear which priorities came from the mentoring conversations.

| Priority / theme | Evidence or gap to validate | Next action and proof of progress | Horizon | Learning resources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One focused theme per row | What is known and what remains uncertain | A bounded lesson, lab, project, or explanation the mentee can demonstrate | Only estimate when study time is known; otherwise “to agree” | Up to three current links, TBX first when relevant, with provider and access note |

Keep only the themes that match the career direction. Possible themes include Linux, networking, Python/scripting, Git, CI/CD, containers, cloud, IaC, observability/SRE, security, or practical AI. Do not include every theme by default.

When the target role or available study time is not known, use one or two conditional priorities and a question for the next session instead of a detailed month-by-month roadmap. After the target is agreed, show a small number of ordered stages with dependencies and practical outcomes.

## Page 3 — Career, conversation notes, and commitments (optional)

Use this page only if there are meaningful career/soft-skill observations, enough session decisions to track, or a handoff to another mentor.

### Career and professional practice

| Topic | Evidence / mentor note | Suggested action or question |
|---|---|---|
| CV and LinkedIn | Specific content discussed or “not reviewed yet” | One concrete edit or a prompt to review together |
| English | Self-report or observed evidence, or “not assessed” | Goal related to stated career direction; no proficiency guess |
| AI use | Example of current use or “not assessed” | Safe, verifiable practice relevant to the target |
| Soft skills | Mentor's concrete observation or mentee-reported situation | Action or neutral question; no personality labels |

For mentor handoff, prompts can include: “How do you currently ask for feedback?”, “What project impact would you like to communicate more clearly?”, and “Would a regular one-on-one with your manager help clarify expectations?” Include only prompts relevant to the conversation.

### Agreed actions

| Action | Owner | Target date / check-in |
|---|---|---|
| Specific commitment agreed in the session | Mentee / mentor | Date or “to agree” |

Do not turn a suggested action into an agreement unless the mentee and mentor actually agreed to it.

## Wording examples

- **Evidence:** “The CV describes maintaining Linux systems and automating recurring tasks with Python.”
- **Inference:** “This suggests practical operations experience; confirm the level of independent ownership in the next session.”
- **Unknown:** “The supplied material does not identify a target cloud provider.”
- **Preliminary gap:** “Cloud networking is a useful area to validate for the stated SRE direction; current depth is not established by the CV.”
- **Resource note:** “Free learning material; certification exam is paid (access checked YYYY-MM-DD).” Include this distinction only when confirmed by the linked source.

## Footer

Use a small footer with page number and “Mentoria — atualizado YYYY-MM-DD”. Add an evidence/source note only when needed to distinguish CV/LinkedIn facts from session observations. Do not include private contact details or a generic legal disclaimer.
