# Planning a series

- **Start broad, go niche later.** The first shorts are wide overviews the audience already
  cares about (cloud computing, DevOps, Docker, Kubernetes, Linux, Git, APIs, AI agents),
  with a light, funny hook. Specific topics (an error code, one CLI flag) come later.
- **Each plan item has:** number, title as the creator would say it (a spoken sentence with
  energy, never the AI template "O que é X: até sua avó vai entender"), area, the analogy
  with its de-para pairs, the real artefact (which command will be run), and the CTA joke.
- **Order** goes from the base (the server, the code) to scale and cloud, then operations and
  security, closing with AI (the hottest topic).
- Keep a few reserves in the plan so a rejected analogy can be swapped without replanning.
- Save the plan as `plano-shorts-<a>-a-<b>.md` in the series folder and get it approved
  before writing scripts; in batch mode, produce in the plan's order.
- The creator's own decks and lessons on the topic are read before the script; they often
  already contain the analogy and the meme the audience knows.
